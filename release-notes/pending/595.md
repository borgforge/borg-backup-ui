### Improvements

- **Back up Docker containers one after another:** A new job option stops each container only while its own folders are archived and restarts it right away, instead of keeping all containers stopped for the whole backup. (#595)
    - The folders come from the writable volume mappings inside the backup sources, including Unraid pool and disk views of the same share. Containers that share folders are stopped together; containers without folders in the sources keep running.
    - Each container group gets its own archive `<prefix>@<container>-<date>` and its own retention series. Remaining paths and database dumps follow in the regular archive. Browse & Restore lists these archives for the job, and the flow preview shows the planned groups.
