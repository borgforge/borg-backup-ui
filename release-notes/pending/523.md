### Bug Fixes

- Information, warnings and errors no longer push page controls down. Action feedback appears in a compact panel at the bottom right. Information and success messages close after 5 seconds, warnings after 12 seconds; longer texts receive up to 30 seconds. Reading with the pointer or keyboard focus pauses dismissal. Errors stay until dismissed or replaced. Form validation and confirmation dialogs keep their existing placement. (#523)
- Restore Tests keeps Save and Test now side by side in both languages. Saving and starting show progress inside the buttons; feedback no longer adds a line that shifts the table. (#523)
