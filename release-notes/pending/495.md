### Before updating

- **Previous job and profile exports can no longer be imported.** Create fresh exports after successful migration. **Existing Borg backup archives remain usable and do not need to be recreated.** (#495)

### Improvements

- Existing job settings, icons and colors are carried over during migration. New jobs start with a neutral archive icon unless you choose another one. (#495)
- Large-archive restore dry-runs select their sampling mode by archive size. The default sample target is 5% of files, capped at 1,000 files; smaller archives are checked in full. (#495)
- Historical records that cannot be matched to a job are kept on disk and excluded from current statistics. (#495)
