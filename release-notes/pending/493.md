### Improvements

- Schedule restore tests per job at a daily, weekly or monthly time. Saving a plan installs its cron trigger; the planning page shows whether cron is active and the next scheduled start. Test evidence validity is configured separately. (#493)

### Bug Fixes

- Prevent backups and restore tests from using the same repository concurrently. The later operation is skipped with a recorded reason; skipped tests preserve the previous verification result. Scheduled tests use the job's test level without being blocked by the former global interval. (#493)

### Migration and compatibility

- Existing interval schedules and reports are preserved. Jobs that previously only had a scheduled policy without a cron trigger now show "Set a schedule"; choose an execution time under Restore Tests. Missed or busy runs are not automatically retried. (#493)
